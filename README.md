# MG Luxury Renovations

Create a premium, high-end website for “MG Contractor Projects”, a Texas-based renovation and remodeling contractor.

Business info:
• Tagline: From small fixes to full renovations — we’ve got you covered.
• Services: Home Repairs, Remodeling, Renovations, Interior & Exterior Improvements
• Location: Lewisville, TX 75067
• Phone: +1 210-838-1594
• Email: menojgarcia32@gmail.com

Design a luxury contractor website that feels like a professional construction agency, NOT a generic template. Use a sophisticated dark charcoal + warm white + copper/gold accent style, bold typography, large visuals, premium spacing and subtle glass/gradient details.

Sections:
1. Minimal premium navbar
2. Cinematic hero with construction/renovation background video + strong CTA
3. Services with large image cards
4. Full-width renovation video showcase
5. “From Fixes to Full Renovations” section with image + text
6. Before/After image gallery
7. Project showcase with mixed images and short videos
8. Full-screen video section with overlay message
9. Why Choose MG Contractor Projects
10. Testimonials
11. Strong “Start Your Project” CTA
12. Premium footer with contact details

Mix videos, project images and clean editorial layouts throughout the page so it feels dynamic and never boring.

Use autoplay, muted, loop and lazy-loaded videos. Add smooth scrolling, subtle reveal animations, elegant hover effects and polished transitions.

Make it fully responsive, fast and lightweight. Avoid unnecessary libraries and heavy animations. The final website must look expensive, cinematic and highly professional — not like a basic contractor website.
